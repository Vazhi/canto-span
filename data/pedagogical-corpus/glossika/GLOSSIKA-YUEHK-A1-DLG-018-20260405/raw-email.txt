YUE_HK • A1

邊個好啲?

Which One Is Better?

Dialog

志偉: 詩敏，你飲咖啡定奶茶好啲？

/si1 man5, nei5 jam2 gaa3 fe1 ding6 naai5 caa4 hou2 di1?/

詩敏: 我鍾意飲奶茶多啲。你呢？

/ngo5 zung1 ji3 jam2 naai5 caa4 do1 di1. nei5 ne1?/

志偉: 我都係鍾意奶茶。點解呀？

/ngo5 dou1 hai6 zung1 ji3 naai5 caa4. dim2 gaai2 aa3?/

詩敏: 因為奶茶香啲，你覺得呢？

/jan1 wai6 naai5 caa4 hoeng1 di1, nei5 gok3 dak1 ne1?/

志偉: 我同意。咁凍飲定熱飲好？

/ngo5 tung4 ji3. gam2 dung3 jam2 ding6 jit6 jam2 hou2?/

詩敏: 我鍾意凍飲，特別係夏天。

/ngo5 zung1 ji3 dung3 jam2, dak6 bit6 hai6 haa6 tin1./

志偉: 但係而家冬天喎，咁點？

/daan6 hai6 ji4 gaa1 dung1 tin1 wo3, gam2 dim2?/

詩敏: 噢，咁熱飲好啲。你去邊度買？

/o5, gam2 jit6 jam2 hou2 di1. nei5 heoi3 bin1 dou6 maai5?/

志偉: 我通常去茶餐廳。你呢？

/ngo5 tung1 soeng4 heoi3 caa4 caan1 teng1. nei5 ne1?/

詩敏: 我鍾意去連鎖店多啲。

/ngo5 zung1 ji3 heoi3 lin4 so2 dim3 do1 di1./

志偉: 點解呀？連鎖店好啲？

/dim2 gaai2 aa3? lin4 so2 dim3 hou2 di1?/

詩敏: 我覺得快啲，你覺得呢？

/ngo5 gok3 dak1 faai3 di1, nei5 gok3 dak1 ne1?/

志偉: 都係。但係茶餐廳平啲。

/dou1 hai6. daan6 hai6 caa4 caan1 teng1 peng4 di1./

詩敏: 係喎，平幾多呀？

/hai6 wo3, peng4 gei2 do1 aa3?/

志偉: 平五蚊左右。咁食飯呢？

/peng4 ng5 man1 zo2 jau6. gam2 sik6 faan6 ne1?/

詩敏: 你鍾意中餐定西餐？

/nei5 zung1 ji3 zung1 caan1 ding6 sai1 caan1?/

志偉: 我兩樣都鍾意。你呢？

/ngo5 loeng5 joeng6 dou1 zung1 ji3. nei5 ne1?/

詩敏: 我鍾意中餐多啲，好味啲。

/ngo5 zung1 ji3 zung1 caan1 do1 di1, hou2 mei6 di1./

志偉: 咁你鍾意粵菜定川菜？

/gam2 nei5 zung1 ji3 jyut6 coi3 ding6 cyun1 coi3?/

詩敏: 粵菜好啲，我唔係好鍾意辣。

/jyut6 coi3 hou2 di1, ngo5 m4 hai6 hou2 zung1 ji3 laat6./

志偉: 我就鍾意辣嘅。咁返工呢？

/ngo5 zau6 zung1 ji3 laat6 ge3. gam2 faan1 gung1 ne1?/

詩敏: 搭巴士定地鐵好啲？

/daap3 baa1 si2 ding6 dei6 tit3 hou2 di1?/

志偉: 地鐵快啲，但係貴啲。

/dei6 tit3 faai3 di1, daan6 hai6 gwai3 di1./

詩敏: 我覺得地鐵舒服啲。

/ngo5 gok3 dak1 dei6 tit3 syu1 fuk6 di1./

志偉: 你係唔係日日搭地鐵？

/nei5 hai6 m4 hai6 jat6 jat6 daap3 dei6 tit3?/

詩敏: 係呀，你呢？你點返工？

/hai6 aa3, nei5 ne1? nei5 dim2 faan1 gung1?/

志偉: 我有時行路，有時搭車。

/ngo5 jau5 si4 haang4 lou6, jau5 si4 daap3 ce1./

詩敏: 行路幾耐呀？

/haang4 lou6 gei2 noi6 aa3?/

志偉: 十五分鐘左右。咁買嘢呢？

/sap6 ng5 fan1 zung1 zo2 jau6. gam2 maai5 je5 ne1?/

詩敏: 你鍾意去商場定街市？

/nei5 zung1 ji3 heoi3 soeng1 coeng4 ding6 gaai1 si5?/

志偉: 商場方便啲，乾淨啲。

/soeng1 coeng4 fong1 bin6 di1, gon1 zeng6 di1./

詩敏: 但係街市新鮮啲，平啲。

/daan6 hai6 gaai1 si5 san1 sin1 di1, peng4 di1./

志偉: 係喎。咁你週末做咩？

/hai6 wo3. gam2 nei5 zau1 mut6 zou6 me1?/

詩敏: 我鍾意睇戲。你呢？

/ngo5 zung1 ji3 tai2 hei3. nei5 ne1?/

志偉: 我鍾意做運動多啲。

/ngo5 zung1 ji3 zou6 wan6 dung6 do1 di1./

詩敏: 你做咩運動呀？

/nei5 zou6 me1 wan6 dung6 aa3?/

志偉: 跑步同游水。你有冇做運動？

/paau2 bou6 tung4 jau4 seoi2. nei5 jau5 mou5 zou6 wan6 dung6?/

詩敏: 有時行山，但係唔係好多。

/jau5 si4 haang4 saan1, daan6 hai6 m4 hai6 hou2 do1./

志偉: 行山都好呀，空氣好啲。

/haang4 saan1 dou1 hou2 aa3, hung1 hei3 hou2 di1./

詩敏: 係呀，不過好攰。

/hai6 aa3, bat1 gwo3 hou2 gui6./

Notes

The choice question 定 (ding6) connects two options, meaning 'or'. In Cantonese, it appears between the choices: 咖啡定奶茶 literally means 'coffee or tea'. Unlike English 'or', 定 cannot be used for negative choices or statements - only for offering alternatives in questions.

The comparative structure uses 好啲 (hou2 di1) or [adjective]啲 to mean 'better' or 'more [adjective]'. The character 啲 functions as a comparative marker here, different from its use as a plural classifier. Note 鍾意...多啲 (zung1 yi3...do1 di1) for preferences: 'like...more'. The structure splits: verb + object + 多啲 creates 'like X more'.

The phrase 你覺得呢 (nei5 gok3 dak1 ne1) asks for opinions, literally 'you feel what'. The particle 呢 (ne1) at the end softens the question and invites response. 都係 (dou1 hai6) means 'also is' or 'also' for agreement - it shows you share the same choice or opinion.

Pronunciation watch: 定 (ding6) has a low falling tone, not to be confused with the high level tone. The word 香 (hoeng1) means 'fragrant' for describing pleasant food and drink aromas - very common in Cantonese food culture. Common mistake: saying 好過 (hou2 gwo3) instead of 好啲 in this context - 好過 is more emphatic and suggests stronger preference.
