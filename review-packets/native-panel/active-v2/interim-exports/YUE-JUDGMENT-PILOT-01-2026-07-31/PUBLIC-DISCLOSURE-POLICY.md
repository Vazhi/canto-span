# Public disclosure-control policy

This policy applies to the public interim snapshot of `YUE-JUDGMENT-PILOT-01` dated 31 July 2026. It reduces disclosure risk; it does not certify that any public survey release is risk-free.

## Threat model

The relevant risk is not limited to direct identifiers. A participant, recruiter, or community member may know that a person responded and may combine that knowledge with rare variety, region, list, completion-time, interpretation, or narrative information. Linked public tables may also reveal a suppressed value by subtraction.

## Public thresholds

- Participant characteristics, recruitment information, list-by-characteristic tables, completion-time extremes, exact response-category cells, and participant-derived qualitative themes require a public count of at least **5**.
- Complementary suppression applies when visible totals would reveal a cell below 5.
- Cross-tabs are withheld when any cell or inferable remainder is below 5.
- Public condition and item statistical summaries require denominator **n >= 8** and may contain only the item/condition identity, denominator, mean, standard deviation, and median.
- Exact rating-category counts, correction counts, fixed-choice interpretation counts, and participant-level combinations are not public in this snapshot.
- A qualitative theme may be public only after an authorized private audit records a reproducible aggregate support count of at least 5 and confirms that the paraphrase is non-distinctive. No theme currently meets that documented standard.

## Linked-table review

The public package contains no participant-characteristic table, region-by-list table, completion-time extreme, exact response-category table, or fixed-choice interpretation table. The condition and item files cannot be joined to participant traits or row-level identifiers. The manifest and verifier reject reintroduction of prohibited columns and files.

## Terminology

The 36 records are a **provisional analytic subset for interim description**. This is not final eligibility or accepted panel evidence. Duplicate, consent, comprehension, quality, free-text privacy, and item-level adjudication remain incomplete until the survey is frozen and audited.

## Review triggers

Re-run disclosure review before publishing any new demographic table, cross-tab, interpretation distribution, qualitative theme, completion-time tail, participant-level data, or revised export. A final package must document the private audit, coding thresholds, source-to-output reproduction process, and reviewer decision.
