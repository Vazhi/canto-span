# Public disclosure-control policy

This policy applies to the public interim snapshot of `YUE-JUDGMENT-PILOT-01` dated 31 July 2026. It reduces disclosure risk; it does not certify that a public survey release is risk-free.

## Threat model

A participant, recruiter, or community member may know that a person responded and may combine that knowledge with list assignment, regional information, completion time, interpretation choices, corrections, or distinctive text. Separate public tables may also disclose hidden cells when they can be joined or subtracted.

## Result-release rules

- The minimum public sensitive-cell size is 5.
- Participant characteristics, regional/list cross-tabs, timing extremes, exact response-category counts, correction counts, interpretation counts, and private-text themes are not public in this snapshot.
- Exact item-level result statistics are withheld, including denominators, means, standard deviations, medians, percentages, and category counts.
- Exact condition-level moments are withdrawn. The only public condition result is one of three broad directional bands.
- Each broad band must remain compatible with at least 10,000 distinct five-category histograms for the known provisional denominator; otherwise it must be withheld.
- A qualitative theme may be published only after an authorized private audit records support of at least 5 and confirms that the wording is non-distinctive. No theme currently meets that standard.

## Linked-table review

The verifier performs an actual condition/item join across all 80 counterbalanced experimental item IDs. It rejects participant-derived numeric fields on either side of the join, requires all 92 item results to remain withheld, and verifies that every public condition band is non-invertible. Extra CSV files and reintroduced historical result files fail the closed package inventory.

## Private-source absence

The five private-source provenance records are fixed. Verification scans repository content by SHA-256 rather than filename, so renaming a private export does not bypass the public-repository prohibition.

## Terminology

The records are a **provisional analytic subset for interim description**, not final eligible cases or accepted panel evidence. Duplicate, consent, comprehension, quality, private-text, and item-level adjudication remain incomplete until the survey is frozen and reviewed.
