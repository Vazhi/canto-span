# Pilot analysis report — public interim derivative

## Status

This report describes an active pilot snapshot from 31 July 2026. The 36 records are provisionally retained for interim description only. The survey is still active, the item-level audit has not started, and no result in this package is accepted panel evidence.

## What remains public

The public package retains the full instrument item inventory and twenty broad condition-level signals. It does not publish exact item-level results or exact condition-level moments.

Most condition signals fall on the more-natural side of the five-point scale. Two condition families are retained only as mixed or context-sensitive signals: human noun phrases before `用嚟`, and `V完咗` with an overt object. These are descriptive pilot observations, not construction decisions.

## Instrument implications

The pilot continues to support several instrument-level cautions:

- broad naturalness judgments should not be collapsed into a binary choice;
- human-subject `用嚟` items need context and interpretation controls;
- completion and perfective forms need minimal contrasts rather than isolated examples;
- subjectless fragments require explicit discourse context;
- corrections and open comments require private adjudication before any public theme is stated.

## Disclosure boundary

`condition-statistics.csv`, `item-statistics.csv`, and `interpretation-statistics.csv` are withdrawn. Exact denominators, means, standard deviations, medians, response-category counts, correction counts, and interpretation counts are not public. Every item-level result is marked withheld in `item-publication-status.csv`.

The broad condition bands are deliberately non-invertible. The verifier enumerates all possible five-category histograms for the known provisional denominator and requires at least 10,000 compatible histograms for every published band. It also joins all 80 counterbalanced experimental items to their condition rows and confirms that neither side exposes participant-derived numeric fields.

## Reproducibility and next steps

The original private analysis script and environment were not retained, so this historical derivative cannot currently be regenerated from source. A future authorized private rebuild must preserve the full source-to-output workflow, complete screening and item-level adjudication, rerun disclosure review, and obtain a separate release decision.
