The optional diagnostic fields were used selectively rather than indiscriminately:

- at least one reason was selected for 91.9% of ratings 4–5, 84.1% of ratings 3, and 13.3% of ratings 1–2;
- a correction or context note was entered for 77.2% of ratings 4–5 and 10.9% of ratings 1–2;
- the median respondent selected reasons on 11 items and entered correction text on 9 items.

### G01 — Semantic type before `用嚟`

Human-NP sentences were substantially less stable than instrument, location, and nonhuman cases. G01D was not simply rejected: one third of responses were natural/acceptable and one quarter were uncertain. Interpretations divided among an omitted-instrument reading, a human-responsibility reading, and failure to recover a coherent structure.

G01C is internally heterogeneous because it combines time, method, document, and opportunity nouns. `朝早嗰十五分鐘用嚟切方包` was weaker than several method/document items, so lexical-semantic differences remain confounded with the intended condition.

### G02 — Surface variants of `用嚟`

All four forms were at or near ceiling. The section confirms broad acceptability but cannot rank the variants reliably. Comments on `係用嚟` sometimes added sentence-final `嘅`, suggesting that some respondents interpreted the sentence as an incomplete `係…嘅` construction rather than objecting to `用嚟`.

### G03 — Generic purpose and actual-use/aspect forms

G03C (`用嚟 V咗`) was the most degraded condition, but 61.1% still rated it natural/acceptable. G03B (`用咗嚟 V`) had the same mean as the generic baseline, while the agentive alternative was highly natural. Naturalness alone therefore does not support a simple categorical prohibition.

Interpretations shifted toward actual-use readings in the aspect-bearing conditions but remained mixed. The “afternoon hour” lexical set depressed every condition and should be replaced or analyzed separately.

### G04 — Complement size, fragments, and questions

The subjectless `用嚟` fragment was usually licensed by discourse rather than rejected: 69.4% natural/acceptable, 27.8% uncertain/context-dependent, and 2.8% unnatural. Interpretation responses mostly linked the fragment to a prior referent or allowed an implicit referent. Matched explicit contexts are needed in a follow-up.

### G05 — Perfective/resultative forms and object omission

`V完咗 + object` was strongly verb-sensitive. Natural/acceptable rates were 90.9% for `睇完咗`, 33.3% for `食完咗`, 75.0% for `寫完咗`, and 50.0% for `搬完咗`. A single pooled rule would hide this lexical structure.

Objectless `V咗喇` was highly acceptable (91.7% ratings 1–2; no ratings 4–5), but interpretation varied: 15/36 treated it mainly as a completed event without requiring an object, 12/36 recovered a discourse object, 7/36 allowed both, and 2/36 required context.

## 6. Qualitative patterns

| Theme | Observed pattern | Analytic implication |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Human NP + `用嚟` | Respondents inserted an explicit object, replaced `用嚟` with responsibility/assignment expressions, or said the object was missing. | Supports structural ambiguity and verb-dependent reinterpretation. |
| `用嚟 V咗` | Corrections often either removed `咗` to preserve generic purpose or rewrote the sentence with an overt agent and actual-use event. | Shows competition between dispositional-purpose and episodic-event structures. |
| `V完咗` | Many respondents removed either `完` or `咗`, especially with `食` and `搬`, while others accepted the sequence with `睇` or `寫`. | Requires lexical-class and regional testing rather than a global ban. |
| Subjectless `用嚟` | Comments asked what was being used or supplied a prior referent; outright grammar objections were rare. | Treat as context-dependent ellipsis/fragment. |
| Lexical variation | Preferences included `程式` versus app/軟件, `溫書` versus 學習/溫習, `生果` versus 水果, `空咗` versus free咗/吉, and `帶遮` versus 擔／攞遮. | Lexical preference can contaminate grammar ratings and should be neutralized or modeled. |

Free-text comments are summarized here only at the aggregate thematic level. The original participant text is not published in this repository package.

## 7. Revision priorities

1. **Randomize within respondent.** The export contains only four presentation-order strings, one fixed order per list. Preserve list assignment separately from randomized item order.
2. **Use multiple controls.** Add at least two strong positive and two strong negative controls. Quality review should combine controls, broad anchor separation, missingness, and implausibly fast completion rather than excluding on one item.
3. **Balance regional list cells.** Use blocked or stratified allocation and delay dialect comparison until each region has adequate representation in every list.
4. **Redesign G01.** Separate instrument, location, time, method/document, opportunity, and human-agent cases into coherent minimal contrasts.
5. **Reduce or redesign G02.** Current items are at ceiling.
6. **Improve G03 contexts and lexical matching.** Use parallel concrete nouns and explicit generic versus episodic contexts.
7. **Retain G04 with matched contexts.** Analyze interpretation jointly with naturalness.
8. **Expand G05 across verb classes.** Cross `完`, `咗`, `完咗`, and sentence-final `喇` with overt versus recoverable objects.

## 8. Interim disposition

Use this snapshot to revise the instrument, document quality rules, and identify targeted follow-up questions. Do not promote or reject parser constructions from pooled means. Item-level, interpretation, lexical, discourse, list, and regional effects remain material.

A later final audit should use a frozen export after the stopping rule is met, preserve all exclusions and denominators, and separately adjudicate any participant text before it is quoted or released.
