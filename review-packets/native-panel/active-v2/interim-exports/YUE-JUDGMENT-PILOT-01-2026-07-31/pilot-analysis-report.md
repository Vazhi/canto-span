## 3. Instrument-design observations

These observations come only from the public rating summaries:

- G01D had the highest condition mean (`3.33`; median `3.0`), so human-NP items need better controlled contrasts rather than a categorical conclusion.
- G02B was at the lower endpoint (mean `1.00`), indicating a ceiling problem for that section.
- G03C was weaker than the other G03 condition means (mean `2.42`), but the public aggregate does not establish why.
- G05B had mean `2.53` and requires item-level lexical controls before a general rule can be tested.
- Fixed within-list order and uneven recruitment prevent confirmatory regional or list-effect interpretation.

## 4. Analyst hypotheses, not adjudicated findings

The condition and item summaries identify candidate follow-up questions about semantic type, aspect placement, fragment context, and lexical sensitivity. They do not determine construction identity, productivity, dialect distribution, or parser behavior. Any later claim must use controlled contrasts, source-linked research, a frozen audited panel, and held-out evidence.

The original report mixed fixed-choice summaries with analyst coding of private comments. That layer is now separated: no participant-derived qualitative alternative is published. `qualitative-theme-disposition.csv` records only broad withheld domains, the public threshold, and the fact that a private audit is required.

## 5. Privacy and reproducibility

`PUBLIC-DISCLOSURE-POLICY.md` defines the minimum cell size, complementary suppression, linked-table review, statistical-summary schema, and qualitative threshold. `manifest.json` records public hashes and the private-source provenance hashes without publishing the private files.

The original private source-to-aggregate script and environment lock were not retained. `OFFLINE-REPRODUCIBILITY.md` therefore records the current limitation and the exact requirements for a future authorized rebuild. The public-side package is verifiable, but the historical private-source derivation is not claimed to be reproducible.
