## Supported propositions

The reviewed qualifying evidence supports:

1. `等 dang2` is polyfunctional and has a permissive `[等 + NP + VP]` use distinct from lexical wait, causative, temporal, notice, and connective profiles.
2. `完 jyun4` is a postverbal completion or resultative element.
3. Postverbal `先 sin1` marks priority or precedence and follows VP complement or object material in close examples.
4. A reference-grammar companion directly attests `等 + first-person NP + VP + object + 先`.
5. `好唔好` can be a proposition-final tag formally related to A-not-A questions.
6. I005 and I008 are compatible with transparent layered composition.

## Contextual rather than generalized proposition

The request or addressee-acceptance interpretation of final `好唔好` in I008 is retained from the source context and lower-weight pragmatic attestations. It is not generalized as the invariant meaning of the tag.

## Unsupported conclusions
