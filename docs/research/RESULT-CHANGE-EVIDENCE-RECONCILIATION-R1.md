# Result and change-state evidence reconciliation R1

## Executive finding

The retired result/change labels do not have one uniform disposition.

Primary research supports a genuine Cantonese **causative–resultative constructional
family**, including adjacent means/cause and result elements. The family is broader than
the old exact `整 + 冧 + 咗` matcher, but narrower and more structured than a generic
English-language “result” label. Competing analyses disagree over productivity and
argument linking, so Canto Span must preserve the disagreements rather than select one
theory as parser truth.

The same research also shows that `變咗做 + result NP` is structurally different from
an adjacent resultative compound: perfective `咗` occurs between `變` and `做`. Under
the diagnostics used in the RVC literature, aspect cannot intervene between the two
members of an RVC. `變咗做` is independently attested, but it must not inherit evidence
from adjacent causative–resultatives.

The seven retired records therefore divide as follows:

- one retired nested predicate, `AA10`, points to a real but broader causative–resultative
  research family;
- two nested lexical change profiles, the inner part of `AA51` and retired `AB77`, are
  independently attested but are not the same construction as the causative–resultative
  family;
- `AB57` is state/property predication plus change/perfective `咗` and independent
  focus, quantity, and particle material;
- `AB62` is stance, time, weather-event cessation, aspect, and discourse composition,
  with a genuine unresolved word-order/valency question around `停咗雨`;
- outer records `AA09`, `AA51`, and `AB76` contribute no additional constructional
  boundary beyond their nested predicate and independently typed clause material.

No retired UUID should be restored automatically. No new UUID should be allocated by
this findings pass.

## Research question

Do the preserved directions associated with `AA09`, `AA10`, `AA51`, `AB57`, `AB62`,
`AB76`, and `AB77` map to:

1. one Cantonese causative–resultative construction with independently typed
   subconstructions;
2. several distinct result/change constructions;
3. transparent composition for some retired wrappers; or
4. unsupported runtime-specific bundles?

The review distinguishes four evidential questions:

1. **Does the surface string occur?**
2. **Does a source analyze it as a causative–resultative or another construction?**

4. **How stable are the judgements across source method and speaker population?**

A corpus token or dictionary example answers the first question. It does not alone
settle the other three.

## What counts as a Cantonese causative–resultative

### Constructionist definition

Lai and Pang define a causative–resultative construction as containing two verbal
elements related to cause and effect and admitting affirmative `得` and negative `唔`
potential forms between them. Their schema covers prototypical resultatives, many
verbal-particle profiles, directionals, and some causatives. They argue that the
arguments belong to the construction as a whole rather than being mechanically merged
from two independent verb frames.

This provides a justified **research-family level** above individual lexical pairs.
It does not mean that every two-verb sequence or every change-of-state clause belongs to
the family.

### RVC adjacency and ordering

Lau and Lee summarize the traditional RVC diagnostics:

- V1 and V2 form an adjacent lexical resultative unit;
- `得` or negative potential material may intervene;
- aspect, measure expressions, and ordinary arguments may not intervene between V1 and
  V2;
- in canonical Cantonese resultatives, the order is means/cause + result + aspect +
  affected nominal material.

Yip's independent aspect study supports the same ordering distinction:

```text
verbal root < resultative verbal complement < inner aspect < outer 咗
```

Leung and Kush's experiment likewise treats canonical homeland Cantonese resultatives
as `M–R–Aspect–Object`, contrasting them with English-influenced `M–Aspect–Object–R`.

These converging diagnostics are decisive for separating:

```text
整-冧-咗-間屋
```

from:

```text
變-咗-做-老師
```

The first has adjacent cause/result material followed by outer aspect. The second has
outer aspect before `做` and therefore cannot be assumed to be the same adjacent RVC.

## Competing analyses and unresolved productivity

### Cheng et al. 1997

Cheng and colleagues argue that Cantonese and Mandarin resultative and causative
compounds involve lexical incorporation, contrasting them with a more syntactic
Taiwanese causative strategy. This supports treating conventional lexical pairings and
wordhood restrictions seriously.

### Lau and Lee 2021

Lau and Lee report substantial Cantonese restrictions based on the event roles of the
nominal arguments. Their central generalizations include:

- a one-argument resultative such as `跑攰咗` is possible;
- a two-argument RVC is favored when the postverbal nominal is both the target of the
  activity and the locus of the result, as with cut-short hair;
- when the postverbal nominal is only the locus of affect, `V-到` is often preferred;
- verb-copying `V-到` and `搞到` provide additional resultative and causative
  strategies.

They do not support unrestricted substitution of any V1, V2, and object.

### Lai and Pang 2023

Lai and Pang use natural-discourse data and challenge a number of categorical
unacceptability claims based on elicitation. They found attested examples for seven of
nine “strong” resultative combinations that Lau and Lee treated as impossible. Their
analysis also replaces fixed subject/object decomposition with construction-level
cause, affectee, and other argument orientations.

### Canto Span consequence

The disagreement is substantive, not an error to smooth away. The project should:

- recognize a causative–resultative research family;
- retain lexical, argument-structure, and register restrictions at subtype level;
- mark disputed combinations rather than declaring a dialect-wide positive or negative;
- require corpus diversity, speaker-background metadata, controlled contrasts, and
  held-out evidence before productive promotion.

## Exact profile 1: `整冧咗間屋`

### External support

The exact lexical pairing is not merely a runtime invention:

- edited and lexicographic sources list `整冧` “cause to collapse/topple”;
- words.hk uses `整冧咗棟積木` in its explanation of Jenga;
- Matthews and Yip give the parallel causative result `整熱碟菜`;
- lexical resources also document `整爛`, `整直`, and `整大`;
- public native discourse contains affirmative potential `整得冧佢`, matching one
  Lai–Pang diagnostic.

The evidence therefore extends beyond the one exact `整冧咗` fixture to a small
causative `整 + result-state` lexical class.

### What is established

- `整冧` is a conventional causative change expression.
- `咗` follows the adjacent cause/result pair.
- an overt affected nominal can follow.
- at least the affirmative potential form is independently attested.
- the profile belongs naturally in the causative–resultative research family.

### What is not established

- unrestricted `整 + any property/result` productivity;
- the complete lexical inventory;
- the negative potential `整唔冧` in reviewed data;
- whether all lexical pairs have identical argument linking;
- regional, register, and speaker-population breadth;
- whether the best final analysis is lexical compound, constructional subtype, or both
  at different levels of abstraction.

### Disposition of AA10

Retired `AA10 CausativeResultPredicate` identified a genuine phenomenon but encoded it
as one exact runtime bundle. Its evidence should be preserved under a broader
**causative–resultative research family**, with `整冧` as a source-linked lexical member.

Do not restore AA10 unchanged. Before an identity proposal, build a source-and-corpus
inventory containing at least:

- `整熱`, `整爛`, `整直`, `整大`, and `整冧`;
- non-`整` resultatives such as `嘈醒`, `着爛`, `跑攰`, and canonical transitive
  M–R–O examples;
- affirmative and negative potential contrasts;
- V-到 and 搞到 alternatives;
- false positives from ordinary serial verbs and lexical compounds with no synchronic
  cause/result relation.

### Disposition of AA09

Retired `AA09 CausativeResultFrame` added only the external subject/clause wrapper to
AA10. The primary literature treats argument realization as part of the resultative
construction or general clause structure; it does not support a separate outer
“frame” node for the exact fixture.

AA09 remains retired. Its subject and clause material should compose with whichever
narrow causative–resultative subtype is ultimately accepted.

## Exact profile 2: `變成 + complement`

### External support

`變成` is independently documented as a lexical change predicate meaning “become” or
“change into.” Source-linked examples establish at least two complement profiles:

- wh complement: `變成點`;
- nominal result: `由窮光蛋變成大富翁`.

The sources therefore support a lexical `變成` change-predicate family broader than the
current exact `變成 + 點` runtime node.

### Constructional boundary

No reviewed source demonstrates that `變成 + complement` passes the two-verbal-element
potential diagnostic used by Lai and Pang. It should not be placed in the
causative–resultative family merely because its meaning involves a resulting state.

The current `AA11 BinSingDimWhComplementVP` remains a defensibly narrow wh subtype.
A nominal-result subtype requires its own source, corpus, and boundary review. The
broader lexical family should be represented in research metadata before deciding
whether AA11 becomes a child, is renamed, or remains an independent narrow identity.

### Disposition of the inner part of AA51

Retired `AA51 DisposalChangeIntoResultFrame` contained:

```text
subject + 會 + 將 + object + 變成 + nominal result
```

Only `變成 + nominal result` belongs to the lexical change-predicate question.
`會`, `將`, the affected nominal, and the outer clause remain independently typed.
No dedicated modal-disposal-change frame is justified by the reviewed sources.

## Exact profile 3: `變咗做 + result NP`

### External support

Authoritative Cantonese lexical evidence includes:

- `個地盤變咗做臨時嘅停車場`;
- `香港逐步變緊做知識型嘅經濟體系`;
- edited CUHK prose with `變咗做紙片`.

The alternation between `咗` and `緊` before `做` is especially important. It shows that
`變` carries ordinary outer aspect while `做 + result` follows.

### Why it is not the adjacent RVC profile

In traditional RVC diagnostics, ordinary aspect cannot intervene between V1 and V2.
In `變咗做`, aspect visibly intervenes. The sequence therefore cannot inherit the
structural evidence of `整冧咗`, `剪短咗`, or other adjacent resultatives.

The present evidence establishes this conservative profile:

```text
lexical 變 + overt outer aspect + 做 + overt result expression
```

The exact syntactic category and function of `做` remain unresolved. It may be analyzed
as a verb, linker, complement introducer, or component of a conventional change
construction in different frameworks. Canto Span must preserve the overt token and avoid
choosing among those analyses without direct evidence.

### Disposition of AB77

Retired `AB77 TransformationResultPredicate` tracked a real and independently attested
surface profile, but its “result predicate” name risks collapsing it into adjacent
resultatives.

AB77 remains retired. A later identity proposal may be justified for an overt
`變 + aspect + 做 + result` change profile, but only after:

- corpus review across `咗`, `緊`, and other possible aspect forms;
- nominal, property, occupational, and place/result complement contrasts;
- negative and interrogative boundaries;
- comparison with `變做`, `變成`, `成為`, and ordinary `做 + occupation`;
- regional and speaker review.

### Disposition of AB76

Retired `AB76 TransformationResultFrame` added only subject/topic and clause structure
around AB77. It has no independent source-backed licensing boundary and remains retired.

## Exact profile 4: `熟咗` and the old result-state wrapper

### External support

Cantonese lexicographic evidence explicitly distinguishes:

- postverbal `咗` marking completed/perfective events; and
- `咗` after a property word describing a changed state, as in `瘦咗` and `靚咗`.

Yip distinguishes lexical resultative complements from outer perfective `咗`, and gives
RVC + `咗` examples such as `食飽咗`. These facts support analyzing `熟咗` as an overt
property/state predicate plus outer change/perfective aspect, not as evidence for a
special clause-level result wrapper.

`熟` is also polysemous across cooked/ripe, familiar, and mastered/learned readings.
Those lexical readings must not be merged solely because they share the same characters.

### Disposition of AB57

Retired `AB57 ResultStateClause` hard-coded material such as:

```text
好多/都 + 熟 + 咗 + final particles
```

The source-supported components are:

- quantity or degree material;
- focus/distributive `都`;
- a lexical property/state predicate;
- overt `咗`;
- final particles.

No reviewed source establishes the whole string as one dedicated construction. AB57
remains retired. The exact `熟咗` lexical/semantic subtypes should be reviewed under
stative/change-of-state and aspect research, not revived as a generic result-state
clause.

## Exact profile 5: `好似今日停咗雨`

### External support and variation

Authoritative lexicographic evidence gives the canonical weather cessation example:

```text
場雨終於停咗
```

with the rain expression before the predicate. It also records `停雨` as “stop raining.”
Natural or performed Cantonese discourse additionally attests postverbal variants such
as:

```text
已經停咗雨
等到天光停咗雨
```

The postverbal `雨` is therefore not invented, but its grammatical role is unresolved.
It must not automatically be labeled an ordinary patient object merely because it
follows `停咗`.

### Disposition of AB62

Retired `AB62 SeemingPerfectiveResultClause` combined:

- location;
- stance predicate `好似`;
- time expression;
- weather cessation predicate;
- `咗`;
- `雨`;
- outer clause structure.

The sources do not establish that bundle as a resultative construction. AB62 remains
retired. Its evidence divides between ordinary stance/time/clause composition and a
separate lexical weather-valency question comparing:

- `場雨停咗`;
- `停雨`;
- `停咗雨`;
- bare `停咗` in an established rain context.

## Speaker-population and method controls

Leung and Kush show that resultative word-order judgements vary with language contact
and dominance. Canadian heritage and English-dominant speakers rate English-influenced
M–O–R order higher than Hong Kong homeland speakers, while canonical M–R–Aspect–O is
broadly accepted.

Canto Span must therefore record at least:

- childhood variety and location;
- current dominant language;
- Hong Kong, Guangzhou/Pearl River Delta, other Yue, and heritage background;
- auditory versus written presentation;
- whether the sentence uses canonical M–R–O or contact-sensitive M–O–R order.

Publication attestation and one native speaker are not enough to erase population
variation.

## Ontology recommendation

### Justified now

- Add a **research family**, not an automatic runtime node, for Cantonese
  causative–resultatives.
- Treat adjacent ordering, potential alternation, argument orientation/linking, and
  lexical compatibility as explicit subtype dimensions.
- Preserve competing theoretical analyses in source metadata.
- Treat `整冧` as a source-linked lexical member rather than the family definition.
- Treat `變成 + complement` and `變 + aspect + 做 + result` as distinct lexical change
  profiles outside the adjacent RVC evidence path.

### Not justified now

- restoring any retired UUID;
- allocating a new permanent UUID;
- making AB55, AB56, or AB80 the evidence home merely because their names contain
  “result” or “complement”;
- assigning one universal subject/object decomposition;
- treating `咗` as a resultative diagnostic;
- promoting a construction based on publication examples without corpus, panel, and
  held-out boundaries.

## Next research packets

The source synthesis supports five concrete corpus and judgement packets:

1. **Causative–resultative inventory:** `整/搞 + result`, other canonical M–R pairs,
   potential forms, affected nominal placement, V-到 and 搞到 competitors.
2. **`變成` complements:** wh, nominal, property, and malformed/competing profiles.
3. **`變 + aspect + 做` change profile:** aspect inventory, result categories, and
   comparison with `變做`, `變成`, and `成為`.
4. **State plus `咗`:** lexical property classes, changed-state readings, and contrast
   with RVC + `咗`.
5. **Weather cessation:** `場雨停咗`, `停雨`, `停咗雨`, context-licensed omission, and
   stance/time composition.

Each packet must include genuine, false-positive, ambiguous, and unusable outcomes and
must preserve source and speaker provenance.

## Resolution of issue #267

The primary-source question posed by #267 is resolved at the evidence-disposition level:

- a Cantonese causative–resultative research family is justified;
- `整冧` is a genuine lexical member but the retired exact matcher is not the family;
- `變成` and `變 + aspect + 做` require separate lexical change profiles;
- the outer retired wrappers remain retired;
- `熟咗` and the old stance/weather bundle decompose, with bounded lexical questions
  preserved;
- no identity, status, runtime, test, survey, release, or deployment change is made by
  this findings document.
