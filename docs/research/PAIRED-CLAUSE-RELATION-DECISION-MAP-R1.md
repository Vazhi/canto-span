## Cross-unit rules

- `ClauseRelationGraph`, `ClauseRelationEdge`, and member-span nodes remain neutral parser infrastructure. They preserve segmentation and typed child metadata but cannot independently establish a Cantonese relation or donate evidence.
- A relation can be **identity-worthy** in this report without receiving a UUID. The term means that generic graph structure plus unrelated children does not preserve the source-supported marker dependency and semantic relation.
- Current typed runtime profiles are implementation facts only. Passing tests, root coverage, and internal subtype metadata have zero independent linguistic-evidence weight.
- Complete overt marker pairs must remain separate from missing-member fragments, discourse continuations, homographs, and neighboring conditional, additive, alternative, preference, free-choice, or quantificational families.

## Family decisions

### PRQ2-008 — `只要 A，(就) B`

**Research decision.** Bounded sufficient-condition core is directly supported; the checked implementation-ready core is overt 只要 A，就 B.

**Accepted core.** Overt condition-before-consequence pair with adjacent 只+要 and overt right-member 就.

**Runtime.** typed bounded core implemented; current metadata `conditional / sufficient_condition`; research provenance `PRQ2-008`.

**Deferred or excluded:**

- unrestricted omission of 就;
- alternative consequence markers;
- discourse-spanning or fragmentary profiles;
- wh-indeterminate-plus-都 interaction;
- automatic status change.

**Disposition.** Route to a coordinated identity/composition decision. Retain the current narrow runtime profile without expansion. Route: #430, batch 1. Collision rows: 13.

### PRQ2-009 — `既然 A，不如 B`

**Research decision.** Direct construction-level support establishes a causal 既然 premise followed by a 不如 advisory or proposal response; broader response types are not established.

**Accepted core.** Clause-initial 既然 causal premise plus overt 不如 advisory/proposal response.

**Runtime.** typed runtime broader than direct evidence; current metadata `premise_response / established_premise_response`; research provenance `PRQ2-009`.

**Deferred or excluded:**

- unmarked response;
- 就 response;
- 噉／咁 response;
- 等我 response;
- arbitrary inference, decision, request, imperative, or question responses;
- temporal 自從 sense.

**Disposition.** Audit and narrow the current response-marker inventory before any identity or runtime expansion. Route: #432, batch 3. Collision rows: 13.

### PRQ2-010 — `除咗 A (之外)，B`

**Research decision.** One source-backed family has additive and restrictive/exclusive continuation profiles; the continuation determines the interpretation.

**Accepted core.** 除咗 introduces a base or exception; optional 之外 precedes an overt additive or restrictive continuation.

**Runtime.** no dedicated relation.

**Deferred or excluded:**

- 除非 exception conditionals;
- removal verb 除 plus perfective 咗;
- formal 除 A 外 variants;
- bare frame fragments;
- one fixed English gloss for both profiles.

**Disposition.** Specify one family with two explicit profiles, exact marker spans, and later bounded implementation. Route: #431, batch 2. Collision rows: 14.

### PRQ2-011 — `唔單止 A，仲／而且仲 B (+ 添)`

**Research decision.** The paired not-only/additional-member relation is directly supported and must remain distinct from standalone or temporal 仲.

**Accepted core.** Shared-domain 唔單止 first member plus 仲 or 而且仲 second member, with optional final 添.

**Runtime.** no dedicated relation.

**Deferred or excluded:**

- standalone additive 仲;
- temporal still 仲;
- discourse formula 唔單止係噉;
- omitted or later-supplied second member;
- unchecked 唔只／唔淨止 and written forms.

**Disposition.** Specify a separate additive-correlative relation and bounded implementation contract. Route: #431, batch 2. Collision rows: 10.

### PRQ2-012 — `一係 A，一係 B`

**Research decision.** The paired option-selection pattern is source-backed and distinct from suggestion, interrogative alternatives, and 或者.

**Accepted core.** Two overt 一係-marked options, optionally with 就, under one discourse selection domain.

**Runtime.** no dedicated relation.

**Deferred or excluded:**

- A 定係 B questions;
- 或者 alternatives;
- ordinary numeral 一 plus copula 係;

**Disposition.** Specify a separate paired alternative relation and later positive/collision fixture matrix. Route: #431, batch 2. Collision rows: 12.

### PRQ2-015 — `每／reduplicated classifier domain … 都 …`
