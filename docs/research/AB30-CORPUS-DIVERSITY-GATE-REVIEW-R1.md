# AB30 — perfective 咗 plus overt object primary-source and corpus review R1

## Executive finding

The narrow AB30 surface profile is directly supported by multiple independent Cantonese
grammars, formal analyses, experiments, elicitation studies, and natural conversational
corpus data:

```text
lexical predicate + outer perfective 咗 + overt licensed object NP
```

Examples include:

```text
我食咗麵。
媽媽焗咗個蛋糕。
我寫咗一封信。
```

The primary literature confirms that `咗` in this position is an **outer perfective
aspect marker**. It does not encode grammatical past tense, and it does not logically
guarantee that the entire event or affected object reached a completed endpoint. Yip's
contrast is decisive:

```text
我寫咗一封信，但未寫完。
“I wrote a letter, but have not finished writing it.”
```

Completion, boundedness, result, and current relevance remain frequent or
source-discussed interpretations. The correct project conclusion is not that `咗` lacks
those meanings, but that the narrow structural node must not assert one interpretation
as an invariant entailment.

The literature also establishes a layered postverbal order:

```text
verbal root < resultative complement < inner aspect < outer aspect < other outer material
```

Consequently, valid Cantonese profiles such as:

```text
食飽咗麵
食完咗麵
嚇親咗佢
```

are source-supported siblings, not malformed AB30 positives. The direct AB30 subtype
remains defensibly narrow when it requires no overt resultative or inner-aspect material
between the lexical predicate and `咗`.

The repository already contains three complete, provenance-preserving corpus packets
with **232 classified candidates**:

- 123 genuine;
- 103 false positive;
- 6 ambiguous;
- 0 unusable.

This is sufficient for the repository's bounded **reviewed-corpus criterion** once the
three packets are accepted through one aggregate manifest. It does not establish
independent second-corpus replication, unrestricted lexical productivity, frequency,
dialect-wide naturalness, or promotion readiness.

AB30's identity can be retained unchanged as a narrow direct-verb subtype. No runtime,
identity, status, fixture, panel, survey, held-out, release, or deployment change is made
in this findings issue.

## Research questions

1. Is simple `V + 咗 + overt object` independently supported as a Cantonese perfective
   profile?
2. What does perfective `咗` contribute, and what does it not guarantee?
3. How should the direct profile be separated from resultative, inner-aspect, motion,
   objectless, experiential, progressive, duration, frequency, threshold, and
   postverbal-subject structures?
4. Do the existing classified corpus packets satisfy the narrow reviewed-corpus gate?
5. What source and corpus diversity remains unestablished?

## Primary source base

The proposition ledger is:

`docs/research/AB30-PERFECTIVE-OBJECT-PRIMARY-SOURCE-LEDGER-R1.tsv`.

### Fan and Chan 2022

Fan and Chan provide a detailed comparison of Hong Kong Cantonese `咗` and Beijing
Mandarin verbal `了`. They analyze Cantonese `咗` as perfective and report that:

- its realis and irrealis distribution differs from Mandarin `了`;
- it occurs in strong VO structures with substantial postverbal material;
- it is disfavored as a sentence-final substitute for the verbal marker;
- in the complex VP profiles they study, it attaches to the first verb;
- `V + 啦` and `V + 咗 + 啦` must be distinguished.

The paper reviews completion, boundedness, temporal, relative-past, and current-relevance
analyses rather than reducing `咗` to one English equivalent. Canto Span should preserve
that theoretical range.

Its “first verb” finding does not erase the independently documented order in
resultative and inner-aspect clusters. The correct constructional unit differs by
profile: a simple lexical predicate can directly host `咗`, while a resultative or
inner-aspect predicate can form a larger verbal unit before outer `咗` attaches.

### Sio and Bond 2025

Sio and Bond classify `咗` as an outer perfective aspect marker and give the exact simple
profile:

```text
我食咗麵。
```

They contrast it with:

```text
我食飽(咗)麵。
我食完(咗)麵。
```

Their analysis distinguishes:

- simple verb + outer aspect;
- verb + individual-oriented inner/result material + outer aspect; and
- verb + event-oriented inner aspect + outer aspect.

The stated ordering is `V–IAP–OAP`. This directly supports AB30's simple subtype and its
exclusion of overt inner/result material from the same narrow identity. It does not make
the excluded siblings ungrammatical.

### Yip 2025

Yip's structural template is:

```text
VERBAL ROOT < RVC < inner aspect < outer aspect < 晒
```

The paper distinguishes resultative verbal complements, inner aspect, and outer
perfective `咗` using suffixation and verb-doubling diagnostics. It provides two crucial
AB30 boundaries.

First, outer `咗` can follow an RVC or inner-aspect element:

```text
食飽咗
食完咗
```

Second, perfective viewpoint does not entail full event completion:

```text
我寫咗一封信，但未寫完。
```

AB30 therefore records a viewpoint-aspect relation over a simple object-bearing
predicate. It must not create an additional completive or result node merely from `咗`.

### Matthews and Yip

The comprehensive grammar treats `咗` separately from progressive, continuous,
experiential, delimitative, habitual, inchoative, directional, resultative, quantifying,
and adversative postverbal material. Basic Cantonese supplies multiple ordinary
`V–咗–object` examples and states that verbal aspect morphology does not locate an event
in past time by itself.

These edited sources support the basic learner-visible profile while preserving the
larger postverbal system.

### Experimental and acquisition evidence

Wong, Stokes, Fletcher, and colleagues provide three independent types of usage
evidence:

1. controlled auditory experiments with matched `gan2` and `zo2` sentences, including
   `媽媽焗咗個蛋糕`;
2. elicited child descriptions in which `咗` follows simple transitive verbs and
   resultative compounds; and
3. natural conversational samples measuring collocational diversity across predicate
   types.

These studies establish that the order is not limited to textbook examples and that
`咗` occurs with more than one lexical predicate class. Child and experimental evidence
still cannot establish unrestricted adult productivity or the complete object system.

## Meaning of the AB30 node

### Encoded

- an overt lexical predicate;
- overt outer perfective `咗` immediately following that predicate in the narrow subtype;
- an overt independently licensed object NP;
- the predicate-object relation preserved from visible structure;
- perfective viewpoint metadata attributed to `咗`.

### Not encoded as an invariant

- grammatical past tense;
- full completion of the activity;
- exhaustion or total affectedness of the object;
- current relevance;
- result-state achievement;
- discourse completion;
- one universal telicity value;
- unrestricted lexical compatibility;
- an inferred or hidden object.

Those interpretations may be supplied by lexical aspect, result or inner-aspect
material, quantification, context, particles, discourse, or a higher construction.

## Direct subtype versus valid sibling profiles

### Direct AB30 positive

```text
V + 咗 + overt object NP
```

The overt object must be independently typed. Corpus examples demonstrate personal
pronouns, possessive onsets, demonstratives, classifier NPs, quantified nominals,
document and utterance objects, headless amounts, and headless relatives. Those object
subtypes retain their own internal analyses.

### Resultative or inner-aspect before `咗`

```text
V + RVC/IAP + 咗 + object
```

These are valid source-supported profiles but not the same direct subtype. They belong
to independently typed resultative or inner-aspect structures with outer perfective
composition.

AB30 must not:

- delete the intervening material;
- treat the first lexical verb as immediately adjacent to `咗`;
- transfer simple-V evidence to the complex profile; or
- mark the sentence malformed merely because it is outside AB30.

### Objectless `V–咗`

Objectless perfective clauses are valid Cantonese where predicate valency and discourse
permit them. They are excluded only because AB30 requires an overt object, not because
perfective `咗` requires an object universally.

### Motion goal and directional profiles

```text
去咗邊度
返咗屋企
```

A post-`咗` place expression may be a goal or locative argument rather than an ordinary
patient/theme object. Role-sensitive motion analysis takes precedence. AB30 must not
convert every postmarker NP-like span into an object.

### Duration, frequency, degree, and threshold material

The classified corpus packets contain numerous strings in which a post-`咗` token is a
duration, frequency, degree, amount, threshold, predicate, or following-clause onset.
Surface adjacency and corpus POS tags do not establish objecthood.

### Postverbal subject and existential structures

Some corpus candidates contain a nominal after `咗` that belongs to a postverbal-subject,
presentational, existential, or independent-clause analysis. AB30 requires an
independently licensed object relation rather than positional inference.

### Experiential and imperfective markers

`過`, `緊`, outer `住`, and other aspect markers have distinct meanings and
compatibilities. They must not be normalized to `咗`. Source-supported inner-aspect plus
`咗` is different from incompatible stacking of two outer markers such as progressive
plus perfective.

### Property or state predicate plus `咗`

A property predicate can combine with `咗` to express a changed state, as in `瘦咗` or
`靚咗`. Such profiles are not automatically object-bearing AB30 nodes. When an overt
nominal follows, its grammatical relation must be independently determined.

## Selectional and lexical compatibility

The structural profile and whole-sentence naturalness are separate evidence questions.

A visible `V–咗–NP` sequence can be structurally transparent while the predicate-object
pair is semantically anomalous, metaphorical, quoted, repaired, or context-dependent.
The project should preserve the visible perfective span and use `NeedsContext` or an
appropriate candidate classification rather than declaring the construction absent
solely from selectional mismatch.

Conversely, passing a structural slot and a lexicon table does not prove that the whole
sentence is natural. Corpus and panel evidence must retain lexical, register, dialect,
and contextual limitations.

## Diversity assessment

### Established

- **Analytical source diversity:** formal aspect studies, descriptive grammars,
  experimental research, acquisition studies, and clinical language-sample analysis.
- **Corpus collection diversity:** one established conversational corpus plus two small
  separately supplied natural-conversation sources.
- **file diversity:** the `r` and `m` tracks span 46 and 44 HKCanCor files respectively;
  their union must be computed from source IDs rather than obtained by adding those
  overlapping counts.
- **predicate diversity:** several verb and predicate classes occur with outer `咗` in
  published research and corpus data.
- **object-onset diversity:** pronouns, demonstratives, possessives, classifier NPs,
  quantified nominals, and additional licensed nominal structures.
- **negative-boundary diversity:** motion, duration, frequency, degree, threshold,
  following-clause, postverbal-subject, existential, and nontransitive competitors.

### Not established

- a second independently reviewed established corpus distribution;
- exhaustive coverage of every possible post-`咗` nominal annotation in HKCanCor;
- unrestricted lexical or argument-structure productivity;
- frequency or prevalence estimates for AB30;
- dialect-wide or register-wide naturalness;
- semantic resolution of the six ambiguous corpus rows;
- a clean role-neutral panel threshold;
- held-out validation;
- promotion readiness.

## Corpus-gate decision

The repository's reviewed-corpus criterion asks whether the selected evidence inventory
is explicitly bounded, provenance-preserving, fully classified, and internally
accounted for. The three component packets meet that criterion.

Therefore:

**AB30 has sufficient reviewed corpus evidence for the narrow direct-V perfective-object
profile once the three complete component packets are accepted through one canonical
aggregate manifest.**

This does not make the stronger independent-corpus-replication field true. The metadata
should distinguish:

- `reviewed_corpus_gate_satisfied`;
- `independent_corpus_replication_satisfied`;
- `corpus_breadth_limitations`.

Another broad extraction from the same HKCanCor distribution is not the next efficient
research action. The next empirical additions should be:

1. an independent established corpus or collection;
2. clean role-neutral panel contrasts;
3. held-out lexical and structural boundaries; and
4. focused adjudication of the six existing ambiguous rows where additional context can
   genuinely resolve them.

## Final disposition

- Simple `V + 咗 + overt licensed object`: **directly primary-source supported**.
- `咗` category: **outer perfective viewpoint aspect**.
- Past tense entailment: **not supported**.
- Full event completion entailment: **not supported as an invariant**.
- Completion, boundedness, result, and current-relevance interpretations:
  **contextually and theoretically relevant, not denied**.
- Resultative/inner aspect + `咗` + object: **valid independent sibling profiles**.
- Objectless perfective: **valid sibling outside the overt-object subtype**.
- Motion goal, duration, frequency, degree, threshold, postverbal-subject, existential,
  and following-clause material: **must not be converted to objects from adjacency**.
- AB30 identity: **retain unchanged as a defensibly narrow direct-verb subtype**.
- Reviewed corpus gate: **sufficient after aggregate-manifest implementation**.
- Independent corpus replication: **not established**.
- Linguistic status: **unchanged `research_pending`**.
