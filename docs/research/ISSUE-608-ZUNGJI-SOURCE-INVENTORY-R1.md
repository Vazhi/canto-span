# ISSUE-608 鍾意 complement-profile source inventory R1

Parent issue: #608  
Work claim: #609  
Date: 2026-08-05

## Scope

This inventory grades sources relevant to the narrow accepted AB33 profile, `overt subject + 鍾意 + visible VP complement`, and to adjacent forms that the current broad runtime label `PreferenceVP` may encounter.

It does not treat every expression containing `鍾意` as AB33. It separates:

- lexical `鍾意 + NP`;
- AB33 `鍾意 + VP/activity`;
- possible clausal or discourse complements;
- negation and A-not-A;
- aspect-marked main-verb use;
- topic, ellipsis, alternative-choice, and scalar composition.

## Verified example inventory

### NP objects

The following source examples establish ordinary lexical-object preference:

- `佢好鍾意音樂` / “He likes music” — Yip and Matthews 2000, PDF p. 33.
- `我最鍾意蕭邦嘅音樂` / “I like Chopin’s music best” — Yip and Matthews 2000, PDF p. 130.
- `我麻麻哋鍾意呢首歌` / “I’m not too keen on this song” — Matthews and Yip 2011 website, Chapter 2.
- `邊個會鍾意呢種人` / “Who would like a person like that?” — Matthews and Yip 2011 website, Chapter 3.
- `佢鍾意咗個學生` — Chen 2018, example (1).

These examples support `鍾意` as an ordinary lexical predicate with an NP object. They do not make the NP profile an instance of AB33 `ZungJiVPComplementClause`.

### VP or activity complements

The following directly support the narrow AB33 surface profile:

- `我鍾意聽音樂` / “I like to listen to music” — Yip and Matthews 2000, PDF p. 115.
- `佢好鍾意食雪糕` / “She likes to eat ice cream” — Yip and Matthews 2000, PDF p. 130.
- `狗仔鍾意食骨，貓仔鍾意食魚` — Cantonese LARSP example reproduced in the published literature.
- `你哋鍾意食咩呀` and substitution profiles such as `睇咩書`, `飲咩`, and `去邊間餐廳` — CUHK ILC.
- `我最鍾意食花生` — Wong et al. 2022.

The best-supported AB33 core remains an overt subject followed by lexical `鍾意` and an overt, independently typed activity/predicate complement.

### Negation and A-not-A

- `唔鍾意落雨` is directly provided by CUHK ILC.
- `你鍾唔鍾意香港呀` is directly described by Yip and Matthews 2000 and Matthews and Yip 2011.
- `你鍾唔鍾意睇戲呀` is directly provided by CUHK ILC.
- Yip and Matthews state that with a multisyllabic verb only the first syllable is repeated in the A-not-A form, yielding `鍾唔鍾意`.

Therefore negated `唔鍾意 + overt complement` and first-syllable A-not-A are real neighboring profiles. The current boundary `我唔鍾意` tests predicate-less/discourse-dependent material; it must not be interpreted as evidence that overt-complement negation is ungrammatical or outside the lexical preference family.

### Aspect

Two distinct profiles must remain separate:

- main-verb `鍾意咗 + NP` is directly attested by Chen 2018 and is compatible with the inherited Luke-Nancarrow main-verb contrast;
- the Luke-Nancarrow pair rejects `咗` in its particular complement-clause configuration.

The sources do not justify a context-free rule that all `鍾意 + VP` rejects aspect, nor do they justify folding aspect-marked NP preference into AB33.

### Topic, ellipsis, and fragments

- Yip and Matthews provide object/topic examples such as `流行音樂我鍾意，古典音樂……` and a topic example with `廣東歌` before `我鍾意聽…`.
- Matthews and Yip provide a topic chain in which a blouse is established and `我鍾意到痺` omits an overt object inside the preference clause.
- Yip and Matthews provide the reply `鍾意呀` after a `鍾唔鍾意` question.

These are evidence for ordinary Cantonese topic structure and discourse recovery. They do not support a context-free AB33 node without an overt VP complement.

### Alternative choice and scalar composition

CUHK ILC directly provides:

- `你鍾意紅色定係黃色`;
- `你鍾意飲早茶定係中午茶`;
- `你鍾意燒鵝定係燒鴨多啲`.

The overt alternative marker `定係` and scalar `多啲` contribute structure outside the lexical preference predicate. These examples should be analyzed compositionally rather than used to broaden AB33 into an alternative-choice or scalar-evaluation identity.

## Source conflicts and unresolved areas

1. Sources vary in category language: lexical transitive/stative predicate, auxiliary, and complement-taking preference predicate all occur in the literature or coding traditions. The repository should keep the permanent name and parser representation theory-neutral unless a later adjudication resolves the category.
2. No reviewed source in this packet establishes a full finite complement containing an independent overt subject after `鍾意` as part of AB33.
3. CUHK’s `你屋企人鍾唔鍾意你嚟香港呀` may involve proposition/event-like material, but the teaching page alone does not settle whether the post-`鍾意` string is a CP, a smaller event complement, or another compositional structure.
4. No source here establishes unrestricted predicate productivity, dialect-wide equivalence, or population frequency.

## Evidence conclusion

The source inventory supports retaining AB33 narrowly as `overt subject + 鍾意 + visible VP/activity complement`. It also establishes adjacent NP, polarity/question, aspect, topic/ellipsis, and alternative-choice profiles that require separate composition or later research. It does not support the current broad runtime fallback that treats any core containing `鍾意` as one `PreferenceVP` construction.
