## Assumption-level research review

### Assumption A — PRQ2-011 `唔單止/不單止 … 仲/而且` is a real Cantonese additive pattern

Supported as a research family.

粵典 records `唔單只 / 唔單止` as Cantonese “not only,” noting that the later clause often occurs with `而且` or `仲`. 粵典 also records `不單止 / 不單只` with the same type of explanation. This supports the marker family, but it does not settle complete-pair versus discourse-continuation licensing.

### Assumption B — PRQ2-010 `除咗 A 之外，B` can express exclusion/addition

Supported as a cross-Chinese and Cantonese-triggered family, but not fully adjudicated.

粵典 records Mandarin-written `除了` as equivalent to Cantonese `除咗` and gives a Cantonese example using `除咗你，仲有邊個知道？`. General Chinese grammar sources distinguish exceptive and additive uses of `除了/除咗 ... 以外/之外`, especially depending on continuation markers such as `都`, `也`, `還/仲`.

This supports retaining the family, but not a single identity that already covers omission of `之外`, restrictive exclusion, additive inclusion, totalizing continuations, and interrogatives.

### Assumption C — PRQ2-012 `一係 A，一係 B` is ready as one identity

Not supported from this issue alone.

Teaching materials recognize `一係...一係...` as an either/or pattern, but the issue’s blockers remain material: binary versus n-ary identity, optional `就`, lone suggestion/ultimatum uses, omitted alternatives, and boundaries against `定/定係` questions and tentative `或者`.

### Assumption D — PRQ2-033 `又 A 又 B` can be positive-only

Not supported.

The issue identifies direct evidence for negative `又…又`, so a positive-only identity would be under-specified. The family must still decide positive/negative profiles, property/action/modal/nominal-list membership, arity, shared-domain licensing, and ordinary single-`又` polysemy.

### Assumption E — runtime gaps can promote these families

No.

Runtime output is a coverage trigger only. It cannot grade sources, settle ontology, allocate UUIDs, or prove identity boundaries.

## Family dispositions

### PRQ2-010 — `除咗 A (之外)，B`

Disposition: retained as source-triggered research family, not implementation-ready.

Required future work:

- grade all source rows;
- distinguish additive inclusion from restrictive exclusion;
- test omission of `之外`;
- classify continuation markers: `仲`, `都`, `也`, totalizing negatives, and interrogatives;
- define constituent and topic/subject boundaries.

### PRQ2-011 — `唔單止 A，仲／而且 B`

Disposition: source-supported research family, not implementation-ready.

Required future work:

- grade the source ledger;
- distinguish complete pair from discourse continuation;
- test overt adjacent `仲` versus other continuations;
- classify Mandarin-influenced versus ordinary Cantonese variants;
- define polarity and scalar/additive force.

### PRQ2-012 — `一係 A，一係 B`

Disposition: source-triggered research family, not implementation-ready.

Required future work:

- binary versus n-ary options;
- optional `就` placement;
- lone suggestion or ultimatum use;
- omitted alternatives;
- boundaries against `定/定係`, `或者`, and lexical preference.

### PRQ2-033 — `又 A 又 B`

Disposition: source-supported research family, not implementation-ready.

Required future work:

- positive and negative profile split;
- property, action/modal, negative-clause, and nominal-list members;
- arity beyond two;
- shared subject, repeated subject, and subjectless discourse domains;
- first-marker omission and ordinary single-`又` polysemy.
